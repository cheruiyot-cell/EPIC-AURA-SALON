Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

At Epic Aura, we treat your privacy as seriously as we treat your grooming. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have. We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to provide our services:

  • Contact details: Name, phone number, and (optionally) email address.
  • Service preferences: Your chosen rituals, session preferences (Silent, Minimal, Open), and any specific instructions you provide.
  • Booking history: Dates and times of your appointments.
  • Membership information: If you are a member, your tier, billing details (if applicable), and membership status.
  • Communication history: Any messages you send us via WhatsApp, email, or phone.

We do not collect any sensitive personal data (such as health information) unless you voluntarily provide it as part of your service notes (e.g., "shoulders are tight" or "skin sensitivity").

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for the following purposes:

  • To schedule and manage your appointments.
  • To maintain your Style File for consistent, personalized service.
  • To communicate confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups (via WhatsApp or SMS).
  • To process membership payments (if applicable) and issue receipts.
  • To improve our services based on aggregated, anonymised analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Any third-party service providers we engage (such as payment processors or booking platforms) are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

3. Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data is stored on encrypted servers with 256-bit SSL encryption.
  • Access to your data is limited to your assigned therapist and the house director.
  • We use PCI-compliant payment processors for any financial transactions.
  • Regular security audits are conducted to ensure the integrity of our systems.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use secure channels when communicating sensitive information.

4. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct any inaccuracies.
  • Deletion: Request the deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Director at harrisoncheruiyot04@gmail.com or call +254 702 555 093. We will respond within 30 days.

5. Cookies

Our website uses a minimal set of cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Google Analytics: We use anonymised analytics to understand visitor behavior and improve our site. These cookies do not personally identify you.
  • Calendly: When you use our online booking, Calendly may set cookies to remember your preferences. See Calendly's privacy policy for details.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, some features (like booking) may not function properly without them.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Formspree: For processing booking and enquiry forms. Data is transmitted securely and used only for our business purposes.
  • Calendly: For online scheduling. See Calendly's Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp Business: For communication. See WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: For site analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Booking records are kept for 24 months after your last visit.
  • Style Files are retained for 12 months after your last visit, unless you request otherwise.
  • Membership records are kept for the duration of your membership and 6 months after cancellation.

After these periods, your data is anonymised or securely deleted.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
