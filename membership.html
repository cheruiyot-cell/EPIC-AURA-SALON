With Respect, This Is Not for Everyone.

The Executive Maintenance Program is designed for individuals who visit us at least twice monthly and who value predictability above price sensitivity.

If your schedule allows you to book ad hoc without inconvenience, our standard booking experience will serve you beautifully.

If you're seeking discounts, bundles, or promotional packages, we respectfully suggest that our membership is structured around access, not price reduction.

If you're uncertain about your long-term grooming needs, we recommend visiting us a few times as a standard client before applying. Membership will be here when you're ready.
