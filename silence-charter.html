How We Protect Your Privacy

Five non-negotiable standards that govern every member of our house.

01

Confidentiality

Before any stylist, therapist, or support staff member touches a client, they sign a legally enforceable confidentiality agreement. This document explicitly prohibits the discussion of client identities, visit histories, service preferences, or any information observed or overheard within our premises—inside or outside working hours. Violation results in immediate termination and potential legal action. This is not a suggestion. It is a condition of employment.

02

Discretion

Your name, phone number, service history, and preferences exist in a single, encrypted database accessible only to your assigned therapist and the house director. No reception-area screens display client names. No appointment books sit open on counters. When we call to confirm your visit, we identify ourselves simply as 'Epic Aura'—never mentioning services or timing to anyone who might answer your phone. Your data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and permanently deleted upon your written request.

03

The Silent Chair

Upon arrival, you will be discreetly asked your session preference: Silent, Minimal, or Open. If you select 'Silent,' your therapist will perform their work with zero initiated conversation beyond the essential clinical consultation. No small talk. No personal questions. No commentary on your appearance, your day, or the weather. You are free to work, think, make calls, or simply close your eyes. This is your time. We are merely stewards of it.

04

Spatial Privacy

Every treatment room at Epic Aura is a fully enclosed, floor-to-ceiling private space with sound-dampening construction. Conversations do not carry. Phone calls cannot be overheard. The Executive Cut stations are separated by architectural screens, not merely mirrors. For clients requiring heightened privacy—public figures, individuals in sensitive negotiations, or those simply valuing absolute solitude—a dedicated private suite is available upon request. You will not be seen by other clients unless you choose to be.

05

Image & Identity Protection

Many salons build their brands on 'before and after' imagery and social media tagging. We do not. Epic Aura has never photographed a client for promotional purposes, and we never will. Our social media and website feature only our space, our tools, and our craft—never our clients' faces, names, or identities. If you wish to recommend us, we are grateful. If you wish to remain entirely anonymous, we are built for you.

From Booking to Departure

How silence shapes every step of your visit.

01

Booking

Your details enter an encrypted database. No paper trail. No public screens.

02

Arrival

Greeted by name quietly. Escorted directly to your chair or room. No waiting-room exposure.

03

Service

Your session preference is respected absolutely. Staff do not initiate unsolicited conversation.

04

Departure

Visit archived confidentially. No follow-up marketing unless opted in. No public reviews solicited.

Two hours after your visit, you will receive a single, discreet WhatsApp message offering to book your next appointment. Reply 'Yes' to secure it. Reply nothing, and we will not contact you again until you reach out to us.

Spontaneous Visits. Same Standards.

We welcome walk-ins. We understand that executive schedules shift without warning. When you arrive unannounced, the same Silence Charter applies instantly. If your preferred session type is 'Silent,' you need only indicate this upon arrival.

Our walk-in intake form—completed discreetly on a private tablet, never on a public counter—includes the silence preference toggle. Within 15 minutes, you will be in a chair or private room, with the same confidentiality as any pre-booked client.
